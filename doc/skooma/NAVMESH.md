# Navmesh — index

## What it is

A client-side walkable-surface mesh built with **Recast/Detour** from the region's declared physics
shapes and terrain. The unit is the **cluster** (a structure: linkset-seeded connected components over
census AABBs, `.worldfield-plan.md` §3): one `dtNavMesh` per cluster, tiled on the region's global
32 m lattice when larger, plus one terrain mesh per region; region-local coordinates, 0.125 m cells,
built on the General work queue. Two products per tile: **Detour tiles**
(polygons; the mini-map's floorplan, paths) and the **floor sheet** (quantised solid spans per
cell with walkable / sky-open / area flags; Atmo's surface ledger and acoustics probes read it). Plus
**walls** off the same heightfield: wall faces (facing, z-range, roof-edge and landing flags) for
Atmo's painting and drainage, wall segments along floor edges with XCOM-style FULL / HALF / none
cover and ledges for the map; **drop links** per class (`AVATAR` 10 m for movement, registered as
Detour off-mesh connections; `WEATHER` 24 m for roof run-off, table only; no minimum landing area in
either; later, authored hints such as ladders and teleporters); and a per-cluster **level graph** (storeys joined by climb and drop connectors) so
the map draws one floor and its exits, never overlapping floors. Two agent classes,
demand-driven: `AVATAR` (2.0 m / 0.25 m) for the map, `WEATHER` (0.5 m / 0 m) for snow, puddles and
drips.

Never the inherited Havok pathfinding (`LLPathingLib` is a stub in open-source builds).

**Being phased out** in favour of the census plus air cells (`AIR.md`): `SKWorldCensusEnabled` and
`SKAirCellsEnabled` default on, `SKNavMeshEnabled` (the polygon and Detour stage) defaults off since
2026-10-15. Since `nav/solid-raster` (B2) `WEATHER` is no Detour class: one solid raster feeds AVATAR and the air.

**Where it lives.** `indra/sknavmesh` is a static library holding the data model and the algorithms
over it: the census record, clustering, the tile build, walls and storeys, air, rooms and wind, with their tests in `indra/sknavmesh/tests/`.
It builds on llcommon and llmath alone, so reaching for a viewer global inside a build job is a link
error rather than a habit, and the tests need no viewer to run. `newview` keeps the three pieces that
have to know about the world: `skphysicsshape` (what a volume's physics shape is), `skworldcensus`
(filling the data model in from the object list), `sknavmesh` (the scheduler, the queries, the
overlay) and its console, `skfloaternavmesh` with `sknavoverlay` / `sknavpalette` behind it.

## Decisions at a glance

- Recast from vcpkg (overlay port on `v1.6.0` + the #796 detail-mesh crash fix and #763 ref-bit check),
  `al_import(ll::recast …)`, no vendored tree, no DetourTileCache, no `DT_POLYREF64`.
- Clusters, not columns and height bands (Soapstorm's model; the first draft of the plan reverted to it
  by mistake). A skybox is its own cluster, a mall is one mesh with intact portals across its tiles.
- SL `(x, y, z)` → Recast `(y, z, x)` (cyclic, proper rotation, all positive).
- Census reuses the physics-shape resolver out of `renderPhysicsShape()`; part cache + 3 ms time slice;
  phantom / physics-NONE / movers skipped; terrain from `LLSurface`.
- Rulings 2026-10-21: phantom, volume-detect and shapeless parts are kept as visible-only census records
  (`mCollides` false, a box) for the overview's cover test, and nav ignores them: the cluster filter, features
  and probes drop them (`combat/minimap-cover`). The census scans a dirty set (object updates and kills hooked in
  `llviewerobjectlist.cpp`, name arrivals, roles) with a full sweep every 10 s or after a 16 m move; cadences are
  `constexpr`, not settings (`nav/census-incremental`).
- The mini-map is a consumer with its own design and thread: `MINIMAP.md` / `.minimap-plan.md`. The
  navmesh plan keeps only the contract it reads (§6 there).
- Walls and storeys are products of the build, not extra rasterisation: faces and segments are read
  off the raw heightfield on the worker; storeys are a read-only walk of the built mesh on the main
  thread, one structure a frame once it has settled, and the map guards them with a ±2.5 m floor window.
- A storey is the level floor reachable from a seed within a band of its height, not a chain of small
  rises: every stair riser fits inside the climb height and chaining would weld a building into one
  floor. Stairs and ramps are one connector kind; only a drop is told apart.
- Slope partition by smoothing groups on the source triangles, not voxel gradients (plan §4.2b,
  2026-09-25): creased normal groups, treads absorbed into their flight, landings cut, so Recast
  draws each flight as one ramp. Connectors (2026-10-04, `nav/no-stairs`) are one per flight
  (slope plus shallow tread groups, no join across a turn > 30°), ends on the centreline clipped to
  the floors past it; kind by name, then treads, then grade > 30°.
  Oracle in `V:\Scratch\nav-smoothing-groups\`.
- Rulings 2026-09-19: `AVATAR` radius 0.3 m and height from the user's own avatar; the census reads
  the sim's pathfinding properties; sheets 0.25 m; overlay port; cover classified at read time against
  the reader's eye height; no object ids on wall faces; the void around regions (surrounds, overhanging
  objects) is in scope, every mesh has its own F64 origin; drop links per class (`AVATAR` 10 m,
  `WEATHER` 24 m); sim surrounds enter the `WEATHER` class as their **rendered shape**, and since 2026-09-23 a surround with physics also enters as its **physics shape** (solid) where it reaches over land;
  Atmo's surround procedural terrain feeds the census as `TERRAIN` records the day it exists. No open
  questions on the navmesh side as of 2026-09-19.
- Branches: `nav/recast` → `nav/census` → `nav/mesh` → `nav/walls` → {`nav/levels` → `nav/minimap`,
  `nav/sheet`}; navmesh first, the map branches off the navmesh worktree. Atmo consumes later.
